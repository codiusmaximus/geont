Does the ontology help?

The places, regions, and questions stay fixed. Only the ontology changes, from none at all to definitions plus rules. Greater London is the study area: place, then borough, then NUTS-3, then NUTS-2, then London.

Conditions

v0Facts only.
v1Facts plus definitions of types and relations.
v2Facts, definitions, and rules, in English, Turtle, or JSON.
COur reasoner on the same facts. The ceiling.

Distractors are near misses: the right category in the wrong region, the right region with the wrong category, a chain that stops early, a similar name, or hierarchy facts off the proof. A category question has no region to deepen, so its depth stays unset.

Each task also has an anonymised twin. Place and region names become F001 and U001. The type words stay readable.
